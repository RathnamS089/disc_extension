# Disc Deck privacy policy

_Last updated: 31 October 2026_

Disc Deck shows what's playing in your browser tabs and lets you control it. It does this entirely inside your browser.

## What it reads

On the pages you visit, Disc Deck reads what the page reports as playing: the track title, artist, album, cover art address and whether it's playing. That's the same information Chrome's own media controls show. On YouTube Music and YouTube it also reads the titles, artists and cover addresses of the next few tracks in the page's queue.

It does not read anything else on the page: no form fields, messages, passwords, browsing history or account details.

## Where it keeps it

- What's playing in each tab is kept in Chrome's session storage, on your device, and is cleared when you close the browser.
- Your settings (whether the mini player pops up, and where you dragged it) are kept in Chrome's local storage, on your device.

## What it sends

Nothing. Disc Deck has no servers, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share any data. The only network requests it makes are for the cover images the music sites themselves provide, loaded from those sites' own image servers so the discs can show them.

## Permissions

- **Access to all sites:** music can play on any site, so Disc Deck needs to be able to see what's playing wherever that is. It only reads media information.
- **Side panel:** to show the player next to the page.
- **Storage:** to keep the list of playing tabs and your settings on your device.
- **Scripting:** to connect to tabs that were already open when you installed or updated Disc Deck, so you don't have to reload them.
